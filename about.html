What is Blob?

Blob is a mood diary app built by a solo indie developer. It was born from a simple idea: we all experience emotions every day, but rarely take a moment to understand them.

Capture how you feel, add notes and photos, and let Blob reveal patterns you might have missed. Whether it's a great day or a tough one, Blob is here to listen without judgment.

Crafted with Passion

Every screen, every animation, every detail in Blob has been carefully designed and developed by one person. No big team, no corporate roadmap — just a genuine desire to create something meaningful and helpful.

Blob combines the simplicity of a diary with the power of AI to give you a deeper perspective on your emotional well-being.

Your Privacy Matters

Your diary lives on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud storage. I don't sell your data and never will.

The daily AI review is off by default. To get it, you turn it on in Blob and confirm that your entries may be sent. While it is on, the app sends your entries to Blob's server (Google Firebase, United States): the text, mood, date and time, place name, the names of people you tagged, and the number of photos — never the photos themselves. The server passes the entries to Anthropic, whose Claude model writes the review. Entries are sent as written, including any personal details you include. For the review, the server identifies you by a random key created on your device. If you turn the review off in Blob's settings, the data kept for this feature is deleted from the server: your entries, reviews, the AI's notes, and your feedback.

If you sign in to use Friends, your profile and the entries you share are stored on Google Firebase. A shared entry can be seen only by the friends you tagged in it; your profile can be found by your nickname.

The details are in the Privacy Policy.
