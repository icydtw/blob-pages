6. Privacy & Data

Your diary. Your entries are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, copies are also kept in your private iCloud storage so that you can sync and restore them; we cannot read that storage. We do not sell your personal data.

Daily AI review. The review is included in Premium and is optional. It stays off until you turn it on in the App and agree to your entries being sent, and you can turn it off at any time. While it is on:

  • The App sends your entries to our server: the text, mood, date and time, place name, the names of people you tagged, and the number of photos. Photos themselves and map coordinates are not sent. It also sends your time zone, language, region, the time you chose for the morning notification, and the notification token of your device.
  • Entry text is sent exactly as you wrote it. Anything personal you put in an entry — names, places, health, relationships — is part of what is sent. It is not anonymized.
  • To write the review, our server sends your entries to Anthropic, PBC. Anthropic processes them on our behalf using its Claude AI model. Short helper requests (for example, to mark the topic of an entry) go to Anthropic as well.
  • Our server runs on Google Firebase (Google Cloud) in the United States. It stores copies of your entries, the reviews written for you, short notes the AI keeps about earlier reviews, and your responses to each review: answers, marks, likes, dislikes, and any comment you leave with a dislike.
  • For the review, the server identifies you by a random key created on your device and kept in your iCloud, not by your name, email address, or Apple Account.

To confirm your subscription, the App also sends our server the signed purchase record issued by the App Store. It has no name, email address, or payment details.

When you turn the review off in the App's settings, the data kept for the review is deleted from our server: the copies of your entries, your reviews, the AI's notes, and your feedback. If your subscription ends, this data is kept for 30 days and then deleted.

Friends. If you sign in with Apple to use Friends, your profile and the entries you share are stored on Google Firebase. Other people signed in to Friends can find your profile by your nickname; an entry you share can be seen only by you and the friends you tagged in it.
